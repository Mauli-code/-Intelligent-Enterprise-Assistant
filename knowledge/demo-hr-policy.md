# Demonstration HR Leave Policy

**Status:** Demonstration information only. This is not official GAIL policy.

Employees should submit planned leave requests through the organization's
approved HR system before the leave begins. The manager should review the
request, and employees should contact HR when they need help understanding
eligibility, balances, medical documentation, or exceptional leave.

This sample does not define official leave balances, legal entitlements,
holiday calendars, or approval guarantees. Employees must use the official
employee handbook or HR portal for real policy decisions.
