# Demonstration IT Support Guide

**Status:** Demonstration information only. This is not official GAIL IT
policy.

For a common login problem, confirm the username, check that the device has a
network connection, and use the organization's approved password-reset
process. Do not share passwords or one-time codes with support staff.

When escalating an issue, include the error message, approximate time, device
type, and steps that reproduce the problem. Do not include confidential
business data in an unnecessary screenshot.
